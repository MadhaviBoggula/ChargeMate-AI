Charging Safety and Frequently Asked Questions

Safety around chargers:
Park with the charger cable able to reach without stretching. Inspect the
connector for damage, water, or debris before plugging in; never use a
connector with bent pins or exposed conductors. Do not charge during a
lightning storm if the site is unsheltered. Keep the charging bay clear and
do not leave children unattended near cables.

Can I charge in the rain?
Yes. Public connectors and vehicle inlets are designed to be safe in wet
weather. Make sure the connector is fully seated and never use household
extension leads outdoors.

Why does charging slow down?
Most vehicles taper charging power above 80% state of charge to protect the
battery. Very cold or very hot batteries also charge more slowly. Precondition
the battery before a DC fast-charging stop in cold weather.

How long will a booking hold my charger?
A booking is a simulated reservation for a fixed time window. It does not
lock the physical charger or start a session. Arrive at the start time and
use the station as normal.

Can I cancel a booking?
Yes, an upcoming booking can be cancelled before the cancellation cutoff,
which is 30 minutes before the start time. Later cancellations are refused
so the slot stays predictable for other drivers.

What if the charger is unavailable when I arrive?
Check the station availability in the app and look for another compatible
charger at the same station. If the charger is faulty, report it so the
owner can take it out of service.

Battery care:
For daily driving, keep the battery between about 20% and 80%. Charge to
100% only when you need the full range. Avoid leaving the battery empty for
long periods and avoid frequent maximum-power DC sessions when AC charging
would do.
