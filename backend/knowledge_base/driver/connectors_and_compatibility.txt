Connectors and Vehicle Compatibility

Common connector types:
Type 2 is the standard AC connector used across most of Europe and India for
public and home AC charging. CCS2 (Combined Charging System 2) adds two DC
pins below the Type 2 shape and is the dominant DC fast-charging connector.
CHAdeMO is an older DC standard used by some Japanese vehicles. Type 1 is a
single-phase AC connector used mainly in North America and Japan. Tesla
vehicles use a proprietary connector in some markets and CCS2 elsewhere.

AC and DC connectors are not interchangeable:
An AC Type 2 connector cannot deliver DC fast charging, and a CCS2 DC
connector will not charge a vehicle that only supports AC Type 2. Always
match the connector your vehicle supports with the connector listed on the
charger.

Checking compatibility:
Find your vehicle's connector type in the vehicle manual or the charging port
label. In this application, register your vehicle with its connector type and
use the compatibility filter to see only stations whose active chargers
support that connector.

Adapters:
Some adapters convert between connector families, but not all adapters carry
DC power and some are unsafe for high-power charging. Use only manufacturer
approved adapters and never force a connector that does not fit smoothly.

Booking a compatible charger:
When preparing a booking, the application checks your vehicle's connector
against the selected charger. If they do not match, choose a different
charger or update your vehicle details first.
