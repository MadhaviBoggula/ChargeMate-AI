Station Operations Guidelines

Opening and daily checks:
At the start of each operating day, confirm every charger reports a healthy
heartbeat, the payment-free session flow completes, and the charging bay is
clear of obstructions. Record any charger that reports a fault and mark it
unavailable in the network dashboard before drivers arrive.

Uptime and utilization:
Track uptime as the share of scheduled hours a charger is available for
sessions. Utilization is the share of available hours that are actually
booked or charging. A charger below 10% utilization may need better signage
or a revised tariff; a charger above 80% utilization may justify adding a
second unit at the site.

Session review:
Review completed sessions daily for abnormal energy delivery, sessions that
end immediately, and repeated authentication failures. High failure rates at
one charger usually indicate a connector, cable, or network problem rather
than a driver error.

Driver communications:
Keep station notes current with access instructions, parking rules, and any
planned maintenance windows. When a station will be closed, deactivate it in
the system so it does not appear in discovery or accept new bookings.

Escalation:
Log every incident with time, charger, symptom, and action taken. Escalate
electrical faults, burning smells, or damaged connectors to a qualified
technician immediately and lock the charger out of service until inspected.
